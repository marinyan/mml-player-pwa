# Inline chords

`(CEG)4` plays C, E, and G simultaneously for one quarter note.

The suffix may be omitted to use the current `L` value, and dotted lengths are
supported: `(CEG).`. Accidentals and local octave shifts may be used inside a
chord, for example `(C#>C<G)8`.

Only notes, accidentals, whitespace, and octave shifts are allowed inside the
parentheses. Chords cannot participate in ties or slurs. Inside a tuplet, a
whole chord counts as one timed event.
